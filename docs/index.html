Data sources

  • Forbes NBA team valuations — 2016/2019/2022 editions via Wayback-archived Forbes list data; value and revenue per team.
  • BasketNews EuroLeague budget research — 2021-22, 2024-25, 2025-26 season articles (cached once, custom user-agent, estimate ranges preserved).
  • EuroLeague standings — official api-live.euroleague.net XML API, regular season only.
  • JB Capital EuroLeague valuation — official EuroLeague press release, March 2026.
  • Market prices & FX — Yahoo Finance via yfinance (adjusted closes; EUR conversion documented in methodology.md).
  • US CPI — FRED (CPIAUCSL), used only for one real-terms CAGR in the note support pack.
  • Full source-by-source access notes: data_inventory.md in the repository; every methodological choice and its alternative: methodology.md.

Caveats — read before quoting anything

  • Survivorship bias: the listed-clubs index contains only clubs listed today; clubs that delisted (e.g. AS Roma, Sep 2022) are absent from the whole history, so the index likely overstates the historical experience.
  • Window effects dominate: the staggered index (2004→) and fixed-12 index (2012→) tell different stories; over the identical 2012-09→2026-07 window their results are the same to four decimals — the gap is a window effect, not composition.
  • Benchmark forms: club prices and ^SP500TR/IEUR are total-return style; STOXX Europe 600 is shown price-only for pre-2014 context and understates the benchmark.
  • EuroLeague figures are estimates: BasketNews research with a stated margin of error; ranges are kept. Payroll and total-budget definitions are never merged; 2025-26 payroll covers players only, earlier seasons players+coaches.
  • Standings gaps: the 2021-22 season excludes the three expelled Russian clubs; Baskonia disclosed no 2021-22 figures.
  • Valuations are opinions: Forbes/JB Capital values are published estimates, not transactions; valuation publishers disagree (a divergence check vs Sportico/CNBC is pending manual entry).
  • Index membership is a choice: Turkish club stocks are excluded (TRY/speculative noise), TKO's pre-2023 legacy-WWE history is dropped, and US holdcos are never blended into the European index — each ruling and its alternative is in methodology.md.
  • Small leagues: Swiss SB League dropped entirely — no published club financials exist.
