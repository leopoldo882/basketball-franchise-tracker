European Basketball Franchise Valuation Tracker

How NBA franchises, EuroLeague clubs and listed football clubs are valued, and how those values compare with the stock market. Built from public sources; every figure says where its numbers come from.

By Leopoldo Lioy. Market data to 23 Nov 2026; share prices update on the 3rd of each month.

Blue is listed European football, orange the NBA and US sports companies, teal European basketball and grey the stock market, on every chart.

EuroLeague

No EuroLeague club is listed and few publish accounts. What exists: BasketNews' yearly budget estimates, the league's standings and one valuation by JB Capital.

French league

The French league publishes every club's budget and payroll each September. Three of its clubs played in the EuroLeague in 2025-26: Monaco, Paris and ASVEL (in bold below).

Sources

  • BasketNews EuroLeague budget research: 2021-22, 2024-25, 2025-26.
  • EuroLeague standings: the league's open API (api-live.euroleague.net), regular season only.
  • JB Capital valuation: EuroLeague press release, March 2026.
  • French league: LNB/DNCCG budgets 2025-26 and DNCCG accounts summary 2024-25.
  • Sportico and CNBC values as republished by Bleacher Report and NBC Chicago.
  • NBA deal prices: ESPN, Sports Illustrated, Hoops Rumors, Axios; one link per deal in data/manual/transactions.csv.
  • Share prices, exchange rates and the clubs' enterprise value and revenue (fetched 2026-11-24): Yahoo Finance, through the yfinance library.
  • Risk-free rate: ECB deposit facility rate from the ECB Data Portal.
  • US inflation: FRED series CPIAUCSL (one number in the note pack only).
  • Code, data and methodology: github.com/leopoldo882/basketball-franchise-tracker.

Caveats

  1. The listed index only holds clubs that are still listed today. Clubs that delisted, like AS Roma in 2022, are missing from the whole history, so the index probably flatters what an investor would actually have earned.
  2. Start dates change the answer. From 2004 the index grows 4.3% a year, from 2012 12.2%; over the same months (Sep 2012 to Nov 2026) the two give the same result, so the gap comes from the start date, not from which clubs are in.
  3. Forbes values are estimates made once a year, so they move much more smoothly than share prices. Volatility, Sharpe ratios and betas are only computed for the listed series: the same calculation on yearly estimates would make NBA teams look far less risky than they are.
  4. The NBA and football multiples aren't measured the same way. Forbes values a controlling stake and counts things like arena income; a listed club's enterprise value is the price of small minority stakes plus net debt, and Yahoo's revenue figure may leave out player sales.
  5. Club prices, the S&P 500 and IEUR include dividends. STOXX Europe 600 is price only and so understates that benchmark; it only covers the years before IEUR started (2014).
  6. EuroLeague budgets are BasketNews estimates with a stated margin of error, and I keep the ranges they report. Payroll and total budget are never mixed in one series. The 2025-26 payroll counts players only, earlier seasons include coaches.
  7. The 2021-22 standings leave out the three Russian clubs expelled during the season, and Baskonia published no figures for 2021-22.
  8. Valuations are estimates, not prices, and publishers disagree. Sportico's Oct 2026 values are on average 5.2% above Forbes for the 4 teams whose figures I could find published; CNBC's Feb 2026 values are 4.1% above Forbes' Oct 2025 list for 15 teams.
  9. A deal valuation and a Forbes value aren't the same measure: deals can include arenas or other businesses, and some are paid in stages (the Trail Blazers row uses the first tranche).
  10. French budgets are what clubs filed with the league each September, not audited accounts. The loss figures come from the league's own summary of the audited accounts: a €34.0M operating loss for the division in 2024-25, €32.2M of it from the three EuroLeague clubs.
  11. Two tickers changed in 2026. MSG Sports (Knicks) trades as MSGK after spinning off the Rangers, and Eagle Football Group (Lyon) is now OL Groupe (OLG). Both keep their full price history.
  12. Index membership is a judgement call: Turkish clubs are left out (currency swings and speculative trading), TKO's pre-2023 WWE history is dropped, and the US companies are kept separate from the European index. methodology.md explains each choice and the alternative.
  13. The Swiss SB League is left out because its clubs don't publish financial figures.

About

I'm Leopoldo Lioy, a final-year BSc Economics and Finance student, and this is a personal project I started in July 2026. A Python pipeline downloads the public data, cleans it and rebuilds this page; the code, the data and a log of every method choice (with the alternative I didn't take) are on GitHub.

Python: requests, BeautifulSoup, pandas, yfinance, plotly. Share prices refresh on the 3rd of each month through GitHub Actions; the yearly figures are updated by hand.Not investment advice.
